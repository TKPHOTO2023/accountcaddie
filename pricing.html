Common questions

Before you book a consult.

Is there a lock-in contract?

No. Every plan is month-to-month. Upgrade, downgrade or cancel with 30 days' notice.

What if my transaction volume changes?

We review usage each quarter. If you consistently outgrow a plan, we'll flag it and quote the next tier before anything changes on your invoice.

Can I pay in a currency other than Rand?

Invoicing is in South African Rand (ZAR). The currency switcher above is there to give international directors and remote founders a quick sense of cost — it isn't a checkout currency.

Do you work with businesses outside South Africa?

Our compliance work (SARS, CIPC) is built for South African-registered entities. We do support South African subsidiaries of overseas businesses.

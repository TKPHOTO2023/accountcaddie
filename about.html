About Account Caddie

Real accountants,
behind every number.

We started Account Caddie because too many good businesses were making decisions on financials that were weeks out of date, or worse, wrong. We're a bookkeeping, tax, payroll and advisory practice built to fix that — and a member of the New Generation Group.

Our story

Founded on a simple frustration.

Since 2011, Account Caddie has worked with South African business owners who were tired of accountants who only showed up at tax season. We built a practice around a different idea: your books should be current enough, and your accountant close enough, that you can actually make decisions from them — not just file them away.

15+ yrs In practice, through multiple tax-law and SARS eFiling overhauls.
500+ South African businesses currently on our books.
2006 The year our parent group, New Generation Group, was founded.
How we work

Every set of books, reviewed by a person.

Software does the arithmetic. Our team does the judgment calls — reading context software can't, and catching what it wouldn't.

What we stand for

Four things that don't change as we grow.

Clarity

Numbers you can actually read

No jargon dumps, no 40-tab spreadsheets. If you can't understand your own management accounts, we haven't done our job.

Accountability

We own our mistakes

Miss a deadline or get something wrong, and we tell you first, fix it fast, and explain exactly what happened.

Partnership

Your growth is the job

We're not counting transactions, we're tracking your business. A quiet quarter and a growth spurt both change how we work with you.

Precision

Deadlines aren't optional

SARS doesn't grade on a curve, and neither do we. Filings go in on time, every time, or you hear from us well before they're due.

Meet the team

Selwyn Watkins

Managing Director

An internationally experienced finance executive and Fractional CFO with 20+ years of leadership across global markets, Selwyn has raised over USD 380 million across debt, equity, and project finance. He specialises in capital strategy, financial leadership, M&A, and business performance, helping organisations unlock growth and execute complex investment initiatives.

That breadth of experience — from raising capital to steering financial strategy at board level — shapes how Account Caddie approaches every client relationship: the same rigour, structure and commercial judgement used to guide major investment initiatives, now applied to the books, tax and payroll of growing South African businesses. Selwyn leads the firm's advisory work and sets the standard for accuracy and accountability across the team.
